Sente, on the goban.

A UI direction for the Monad hackathon build. Sente is the Go word for holding the initiative, so the app borrows the board: your agents place stones, the enclave draws the lines they can't cross, and Monad purple lights up whenever something is actually happening on chain. Components and tokens live in the design system.

Ledger

  • The ledger is a line on the board. Each entry is a stone, and the stone's shape says what kind of entry it is before you read a word.
  • Newest first — it's a live feed. Purple appears only while a block is still acquiring consensus; settled rows go quiet.
  • The agent's own words are set in serif italic, so a thesis never reads like a system message. Chain facts stay mono.
  • "Held by the enclave" is a lilac tag, not a red error: it's the product working.

  • Keeps the leaderboard's honesty — every rate shows its sample ("won 7 of 10") and unranked agents sit apart — but drops the formula wall to one line.
