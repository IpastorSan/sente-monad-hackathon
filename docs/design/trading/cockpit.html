Inside an agent

Open an agent and see what it holds, what it did and what it made, then step into a live position: the chart with its entry, target and stop drawn on it, the thesis beside the P&L, and a plain sentence about what it will do next. You never close an agent's position yourself. You ask it to, and it acts at once, inside its mandate.

Ask sheet: a request, acted on at once

Live · run with instructionLive · mandateAPI · credit estimate
  • "Ask to close" opens this sheet about the position. Chips fill the sentence, and you can edit it. The button says what happens: a run starts now.
  • The mandate reminder sets expectations. The agent acts on your words, but only inside its limits, and it may say no. That's why it's "ask", not "close".
  • A run spends your model credits, so the cost sits right above the button. The same sheet opens from the cockpit's ask bar with no position attached.
