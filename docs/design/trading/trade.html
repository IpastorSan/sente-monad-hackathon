Place an order

The order ticket is a bottom sheet over the asset page. One giant number, presets, and a button whose label says what's missing or what will happen. Market orders are bounded (a max slippage, and they can partially fill), manual trades are several transactions rather than one, and a perp's liquidation price is our estimate. The ticket says all three plainly instead of hiding them behind a spinner.

Signing · approve, deposit, batch

Limit buy

API · ticker, klinesSigning · deposit, order
  • The limit is a purple-soft line on a mini chart; dragging it edits the field and the "vs market" helper.
  • No expiry picker, because Kuru has none. The sheet says so instead of offering "Good till" and quietly ignoring it.
  • Funds are named: the USDC is deposited and waits in Kuru, cancellable any time from Portfolio → Open orders.
  • This is the order that shows up as "buy 150 MON at 0.9500" on Portfolio.

Limit resting

Signing · order, cancelAPI · open orders
  • A hollow white stone: your move, placed as intent but not yet played. It turns solid, fill by fill.
  • Status comes from polling open orders; a fill arrives as a notification, not a live tick.
  • Cancel is right here because it is the only edit Kuru allows: no amend, so moving the price means cancel and place again.

API · quote, candlesSigning · orderVenue · SL/TP triggers
