ETH-PERP: candles with your levels

API · candles, mark, indexAPI · fundingSigning · long, short
  • Perps open in candles (the line toggle is the icon). Your entry is a white dashed line with a white stone where you filled, so the chart and the ledger agree.
  • At 3× the liquidation estimate sits 33% below; drawing it would squash the candles into a strip. With fit: false the scale follows the price and liquidation becomes a "↓ LIQ" edge chip: still on the chart, never flattening it. It says "est." because it's our computation and excludes accrued funding.
  • Mark vs index side by side: mark drives P&L and liquidation. Your position is one row under the chart; funding says who pays in words, then the rate and countdown.
  • Every figure is AUSD. The range pills skip 1Y: Perpl serves 1,024 candles and no weekly interval.

Stale data: say how old, don't pretend

Live · pollingAPI · per-venue health
  • The breathing mint dot means "fresh from the last poll". After two missed polls it becomes a hollow ring and the label counts up: "as of 12s ago".
  • Only the venue that's down dims. Prices stay readable, the 24h change is replaced by their age, and the reason is one plain sentence.
  • Trading isn't blocked: the ticket re-quotes before you confirm and every market order carries a worst price, so a stale list can't fill you at a stale price.
