Goban / Trading study (SEN-61)
先

Trade it yourself, or hire it done.

A study for a trading-first Sente. People come in through markets and charts, trade spot and perps themselves, hire preset agents to trade for them, and can step inside any agent to watch a position live, with its entry, stop and target drawn on the chart. It extends the Goban system; every screen is annotated with what backs it today and what it needs, so the study doubles as a build plan.

Job 1

Trading is the way in

Markets, a chart you can scrub, a quote before you commit, market and limit orders, leverage on perps, and one honest screen for what filled.

Job 2

Preset agents

A gallery of strategies with a few friendly knobs, a suggested mandate, and live results from agents already running them. No backtests, because none exist.

Job 3

Inside an agent

What it holds, what it did, what it made. Tap a position and see the agent's entry, stop, target and liquidation on the chart, and its reasoning beside it.

Reading the annotations

What backs each screen. The app itself never shows these labels

  • LiveExists in the app or API today.
  • APIThe venue adapters already do it; it needs a read route or a small server addition.
  • SigningNeeds you to trade from your own wallet: phone-verified contract calls and venue onboarding. The biggest new build.
  • VenueNeeds something Kuru or Perpl don't offer on testnet, like native stop-loss and take-profit orders.

Rules the study keeps

From the capability map (capabilities.md)

  • Stop-loss and take-profit are never shown as venue orders. On an agent's position they are levels the agent checks each run, and the screen says so.
  • Market orders carry a max slippage and can partially fill; the result screen says what filled and what was cancelled.
  • Kuru is priced in USDC and Perpl in AUSD. Mixed totals say "≈ $".
  • You can't close an agent's position yourself: you ask it to, amend its mandate, revoke it, or take the funds back.
  • Prices poll every few seconds; screens show "as of" instead of pretending to stream.
  • No backtests anywhere. Preset results are live agents, with the sample size.
