Trading study / Agents

Hire a strategy

The Agents tab stops asking people to write a system prompt. They pick a preset, set a few plain parameters, draw the mandate, fund and hire. A preset is only text an LLM reads, so every screen keeps two things apart: the strategy (guidance, checked when the agent runs) and the mandate (limits the enclave enforces). Performance is always the live record of agents running that preset, with its sample size. There are no backtests here.

  • No robots. Each preset is a joseki: a small slice of the board with the strategy's shape in stones (a band for ranges, a ladder for trends, mirrored pairs for funding). Purple stones are the agent's moves; hollow rings are levels it watches.

  • Guardian is featured because it answers "where is my stop-loss?" honestly.

The catalog

Six presets: the card, and the parameters each one renders into strategy text

API · static catalog {id, name, params, render, mandate}API · get_klines toolAPI · per-agent cadence
  • A preset is {id, name, description, params schema, render(params) → strategy, suggested mandate preset}. Rendering happens on the phone and hiring is the existing POST /agents: no new hire route.
  • Four of six presets read price history, so they need the small get_klines tool. DCA Stacker and Guardian need per-agent cadence; until then they run on the global timer and the card says so.
  • Guardian's stat is the honest one for a soft stop: how far past your line the exit actually filled, across its last 37 exits. It can only guard coins you hand to it; your own wallet stays yours.

Hire Range Trader

9:41●●●
Presets
Range Trader Kuru spot · MON, WETH · every 15 min
Med

It finds the recent high and low of one market and buys near the low. It sells when price climbs to your target or falls through your stop. When price leaves the range for good, it stops buying and waits.

How it decides
  1. Reads MON's 3-day high and low from 15-minute candles.
  2. If price is within 1.5% of the low and it holds no MON, buys with up to 40% of its budget.
  3. Writes a thesis with the entry, a target 3% above and a stop 3% below.
  4. On every later run, sells if price is past the target or the stop.
  5. If the range is under 2% wide, does nothing.
What it will be told

Trade MON-USDC on Kuru spot only. Each run, fetch 3 days of 15-minute candles and find the range low and high. If the mid price is within 1.5% of the low and you hold no MON, buy with at most 40% of your USDC…

What it can't do

Configure · mandate (step 2 of 3)

Live · mandate presets, enclave policy
  • Goban's "Draw the lines", prefilled from the preset's suggested mandate (Standard) and narrowed to the market chosen in step 1. Changing a preset chip keeps the market.
  • Gauges show the strategy inside the limits: the fill is what the parameters plan to use, the tick is the cap. If a parameter would exceed a cap, the gauge turns hot and step 1 shows why.
  • Shield = enforced in the enclave; "sente" = checked by our gate before sending. No drawdown line: the mandate has no such field, so we don't draw one.
