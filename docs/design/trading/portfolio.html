Trading study / Portfolio

What I own

The Portfolio tab answers one question: where is my money, and what is it doing? One total in dollars, honestly marked "≈" because it sums AUSD and USDC; a bar that splits it into cash, spot, perps and capital with agents; then your positions, open orders and history. Your own positions can be managed here. Your agents' positions sit beside them for the full picture, but they belong to the agent, so they open in its cockpit.

Positions (same tab, scrolled)

Signing · your positionsAPI · agent portfolio routeAPI · spot uPnL (price route)

Balances hidden

Live · device setting
  • The eye hides amounts and sizes but keeps percentages, sides and markets. Glancing at a phone on a train shows direction, not wealth.
  • The legend switches to shares of the total, and the chart keeps its shape with no price tag. The toggle turns purple while active so you know the numbers are hidden.
  • The setting is saved on the device and applies app-wide (Home, cockpits, the ticket's balance line), not just on this tab.

Signing · close, addAPI · klines, mark, fundingVenue · SL/TP
  • The headline is unrealised P&L, not price, because that's what you check when you open a position. Its % is measured on margin, so it matches what you put in.

Open orders, with the cancel sheet

Signing · place, cancelAPI · open orders
  • The order card shows how far the order is from the market: the limit as a hollow stone (planned, not yet played), the market as a solid dot, and "fills if MON drops 3.18%".

  • No "Edit" button, because Kuru has no amend and no expiry. The sheet explains the workaround instead.

History

Signing · your fillsAPI · fills (Kuru receipts, Perpl REST)API · transfer history
  • History uses the ledger's stones: a white stone is capital in, a hollow white ring is capital out, a purple stone is a fill, a hollow purple ring is an order still waiting, and a half stone is a closed trade with its result.
  • Every fill shows side, size, price, venue and fee in that venue's currency. The tx hash is set in mono type (the chain's voice) and opens the explorer.
  • Grouped by day, newest first. Your agents' fills stay in their own ledgers, so this list is only what you did.
